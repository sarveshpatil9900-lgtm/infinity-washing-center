Common questions

How do I book a visit?

Call us or send a WhatsApp message. Tell us your area, the machine brand and model, the problem, and a convenient time. We will confirm availability and visit details with you.

Do you come to my home?

Yes, we offer doorstep service. Share your address area when you contact us so we can confirm we can reach you.

Which brands do you repair?

We work on IFB and LG washing machines. For other brands, message us the brand and model and we will tell you if we can help.

What should I tell you about the problem?

Share the brand, model, how old the machine is if you know, and what happens: for example it does not drain, does not spin, shows an error code, or makes a noise. A photo or video of the error helps if you can send one on WhatsApp.

How much will the repair cost?

The cost depends on the fault and can only be confirmed after the machine is inspected. We do not quote a final price from a message alone.

Do you only repair washing machines?

Washing machines are our main work. We also offer installation and repair support for other appliances. Ask us about yours.

When are you available?

Every day, 9 AM to 9 PM. Visit slots depend on availability, which we confirm when you book.
